For foreign companies looking to sell regulated products in Brazil, regulatory compliance is not optional — it is a structural requirement to access the market.

Brazil has one of the most robust regulatory frameworks in Latin America, especially for products that may impact health, safety, or consumer well-being. These regulations are overseen by ANVISA (Agência Nacional de Vigilância Sanitária), Brazil’s national health regulatory authority.

Whether your company is a manufacturer, distributor, brand owner, exporter, or global supplier, understanding Brazil’s regulatory requirements is essential to operate successfully in the country.

## Which Products Require ANVISA Authorization to Be Imported into Brazil

ANVISA regulates a wide range of products commonly imported into Brazil, including:

-   [Cosmetics](/blog/importacao-de-cosmeticos.html) and personal care products
-   Pharmaceuticals and pharmaceutical ingredients
-   Medical devices and hospital equipment
-   Health-related [chemicals](/segmentos/produtos-quimicos.html) and raw materials
-   Food products, supplements, and ingredients
-   Sanitizing and hygiene products

For these categories, compliance goes beyond customs documentation or freight operations. Both the products and the importing company must meet ANVISA regulatory requirements.

## AFE Is Mandatory for Regulated Imports

In addition to product registration or notification, Brazilian regulation requires companies involved in importing, storing, or distributing ANVISA-regulated products to hold an AFE (Authorization of Operation).

The AFE is an official authorization issued by ANVISA that allows a company to legally operate with regulated products in Brazil.

It confirms that the company:

-   Has an approved operational structure in Brazil
-   Complies with sanitary, technical, and regulatory standards
-   Is authorized to import, store, and distribute regulated products
-   Is subject to ANVISA supervision and audits

Without an AFE, regulated imports cannot be legally completed in Brazil, regardless of the product’s quality standards or international certifications.
