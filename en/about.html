About WM Trading

With the client at the center of our business, we expanded quickly: more branches, new tax benefits and a growing presence in the international market. More than obsessed with delivering the best logistics and tax project, we work every day to cultivate our clients.

Today WM is one of the largest trading companies in Brazil, ready to deliver tailored projects for companies across many segments. Our 22 years of experience in successful operations reflect how strongly we pursue our main mission: to be the most complete solution in foreign trade.
