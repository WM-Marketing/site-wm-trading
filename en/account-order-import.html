How does the transaction payment process work?

During import operations by Account and Order, nationalization and product expenses can be paid by WM or the importing company, at their choice. WM acts as the consignee of the operation and you can also take advantage of one of our 7 tax benefits and our reach throughout the country to receive your products with the best logistical and tax conditions! The importer must also be enabled in RADAR and its limit must correspond to the size of the purchase, WM qualification is not enough.

We carry out a thorough check of all documents and requirements necessary for imported NCMs

We can pay all transaction expenses, including the merchandise received

We organize all cargo shipment, regardless of the chosen mode

We take care of all nationalization and customs clearance at the chosen location

We coordinate and contract national shipping to deliver items to the stipulated address

Import Flow by Account and Order

Negotiation with the exporter and sending of the Proforma

WM sends cost spreadsheet for approval

Deposit of the value of the merchandise to WM *

Exchange closing and payment to the exporter

Organization of shipment with the exporter and cargo agent

Deposit of taxes and expenses to WM*

Nationalization and Handover

WM registers the DI and pays taxes

Nationalization and clearance of merchandise

National freight quote and shipping organization
