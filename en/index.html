Order Operation

Understand our tax benefits and avoid opening branches in states that offer reduced ICMS rates, such as Espírito Santo and Santa Catarina!

Commission Operation

In the Commission Operation, WM acts as cargo consignee and manages the import process at every stage. Tax benefits can be used if your company has a branch in SC, ES or PE!

We organize all documents, international and domestic logistics, and handle customs clearance with tax optimization for your company.

Customs Advisory

We handle the entire import process and advise on customs clearance at any port in Brazil.

This option is used mainly when your company holds a federal tax benefit, such as REIDI or Suframa. We also provide advisory for centralized generation photovoltaic projects across the country.
